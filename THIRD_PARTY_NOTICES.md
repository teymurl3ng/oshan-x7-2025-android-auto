## CarLink

Setup downloads the unchanged official [CarLink v1.0.7 APK and source](https://github.com/F-e-n-y-x/CarLink/tree/v1.0.7).
This repository does not modify, re-sign or distribute its APK.
CarLink uses [AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/F-e-n-y-x/CarLink/blob/v1.0.7/LICENSE);
an unchanged copy is included in [LICENSES/CarLink-AGPL-3.0.txt](LICENSES/CarLink-AGPL-3.0.txt).
CarLink incorporates DiPlay and DiAuto; their notices remain in the corresponding
source. The installer enables CarLink's existing USB auto-confirm service; it
does not copy that service's implementation into this repository.

## Factory launcher and earlier components

The proprietary Oshan launcher is distributed only as binary/source deltas for
the exact owner-supplied factory base. Complete factory/modified APKs and signing
inputs remain private. Prior receiver identities retained internally for old
journal recovery do not form part of the current installation path.
Credit to [Open Headunit](https://github.com/andreknieriem/open-headunit), the
previous receiver, and [DiPlay](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay).
